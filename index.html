Integration

A two-way loop, not a one-way export

Results come in from the analysis model, and the detailing goes back out to the model, the BIM and the drawing set.

01

Back into ETABS

Column detailing writes back as one section property per mark, complete with its rebar. Set to check your bars rather than redesign them.

02

Into Revit

The detailing schedule exports as native detail lines, text notes and placed families, editable, taggable and schedulable.

03

Into CAD

Beam and column schedules, sections and layout plans export as DXF with real line weights and text, ready for the drawing set.

About

Built by an engineer, for engineers

Design Suite grew out of real drawings, real review comments and real issued calculation reports, not a generic template. Every rule in it, from bar spacing to how a column mark groups up a building, came from an actual project, checked against an actual issued report.

  • Manually verifiable. Every module opens a formatted, line-by-line derivation with clause references. Nothing is a black box.
  • Your rules win. A hand-typed call-out is always applied and re-derived through the whole schedule, with anything it breaches reported, never silently undone.
  • It leaves as your document. Your own logo and title block on every report, and the model, the detailing and the reports never leave your machine.
